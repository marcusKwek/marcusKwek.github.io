4DOF Arm

Project Dates: 06/2025 - 02/2026

Status: New Version In Development!

Tools involved: 3D Printing, C++, Python, Arduino, Onshape


Features:

● Laptop uses camera to identify parts of my hand with computer vision, and uses various measurements to control the position of the arm

● Stepper Motors power 5 degrees of motion for the arm, and a servo motor is used for a claw

● Arm automatically homes itself to know its location on startup
